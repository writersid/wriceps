# Wriceps real-phone checklist

Run on Chrome for Android and Safari on iPhone (desktop Chrome is covered by automated checks). From SPEC.md "Evaluation criteria", Phase 6.

## Pending iPhone checks (parts 5a and 5b)
- [ ] Log a set, tap Share: the share sheet opens with the card, and the caption with the link arrives in WhatsApp.
- [ ] If the caption doesn't arrive: "Message copied" shows, and pasting works.
- [ ] A spotter game's team card shares fine.
- [ ] A challenge link opened from WhatsApp (in its in-app browser, and in Safari) shows "Challenge: beat N", the same letter or draw as the sender, and the comparison after logging.
- [ ] The Start / Keep prompt works both ways.

## Both phones
- [ ] One set of each mechanic, played through; plus a spotter game, refreshed mid-game with no turns lost.
- [ ] The share card appears in under 2 seconds, is readable, and shows the URL.
- [ ] Close and reopen the browser: progress is kept. Clear site data: the app starts fresh with no errors.
- [ ] From tapping a WhatsApp link to typing in a set: under 10 seconds.
- [ ] Typing feels instant, and the keyboard never covers the line being typed.
- [ ] Light and dark mode both look right.

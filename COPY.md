## Voice guide

Every future line of Wriceps copy follows these rules.

1. **Clear first, funny second.** If a line has to choose, it chooses being understood. Instructions, errors, and the privacy page stay plain. Humor lives in tips, titles, and small moments.
2. **Dry, never cute.** Understatement over excitement. At most one wink per line, and almost no exclamation marks ("New PR!" is the exception).
3. **The gym is the frame, used lightly.** At most one gym word per line, and only where it fits naturally.
4. **A coach, not a critic.** Second person, short sentences, active verbs. A flag is information, never a scolding. Say what to do next.
5. **Written for writers.** Every tip teaches a real craft idea in a few words.
6. **One sentence per tip, ideally under eight words.**

Mechanics: US English, sentence case on buttons, no em dashes.

Vocabulary: a set is an exercise, a rep is one piece, a spotter is the pass-the-phone partner, a PR is a player's personal best, and Top set is the top score band (90 to 100).
